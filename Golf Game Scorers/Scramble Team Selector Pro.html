Balance methods

Greedy handicap balance (default) spreads total handicap as evenly as possible across teams by always assigning the next player to the team with the current lowest total handicap.

Snake draft by handicap sorts players by handicap and deals them to teams in a back-and-forth (snake) order so low, mid, and high handicaps are mixed across teams.

Random, then rebalance starts with a random team draw, then nudges players between teams to reduce big differences in total handicap.

Anchor low caps per team first spreads the lowest handicaps evenly as “anchors,” then fills in the rest to keep teams roughly equal.

Minimise handicap variance runs several shuffled draws and picks the one with the smallest spread of total team handicaps.
